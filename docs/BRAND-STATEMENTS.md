# Lustra — Brand & Corporate Statements

The canonical wording for every statement the company makes about itself.
Use these verbatim in decks, proposals, job posts, press, and partner
onboarding — consistency is the brand. The customer-facing subset renders on
the About page (`src/app/about/page.tsx`).

---

## Vision

> To be Africa's most loved laundry company — the standard by which garment
> care is measured.

## Mission

> We give people their time back. We deliver flawless garment care through
> beautiful spaces, obsessive craft, and technology that disappears — so that
> laundry stops being anyone's job but ours.

## Purpose (our "why")

> Time spent on chores is time taken from lives. Every hour we return to a
> customer is an hour returned to family, work, rest, and ambition.

## Tagline

> **Laundry, elevated.**

## Positioning statement

> For busy households and quality-driven businesses in Lagos who refuse to
> compromise on how they present themselves, **Lustra** is the premium laundry
> platform that pairs master garment care with effortless technology. Unlike
> traditional laundries, every Lustra order is tracked live, insured,
> guaranteed, and returned like new — from everyday wash & fold to couture,
> wedding dresses, and traditional attire.

## Brand promise

> **Like new, on time, every time.** Every garment is photographed at intake,
> cleaned to your saved preferences, quality-checked by a named specialist,
> and returned when we said it would be — or we make it right within 48 hours.

## Core values

1. **Care is the product.** Machines wash clothes; people care for them.
   Every process ends with a human quality check and a name on the order.
2. **Technology that disappears.** The best tech is the tap you didn't have
   to make. We automate the friction and keep the humanity.
3. **Clean shouldn't cost the planet.** Water reclamation, biodegradable
   chemistry, and zero-perc cleaning as the default, not the upsell.
4. **Standards over shortcuts.** Retail-standard folds, 12-point quality
   checks, and SLAs we publish instead of hiding.
5. **Heritage is handled with reverence.** Agbada, aso-oke, lace, and gele
   carry family history. We treat every traditional garment as an heirloom
   in progress.
6. **We show up — with the lights on.** 24/7 power backup, published hours,
   and answered phones. Reliability is the first luxury.

## Quality guarantee

> Every order passes a 12-point quality check before it leaves the facility.
> Garments are insured up to ₦500,000 per item. Report any issue within
> 7 days — in the app, on WhatsApp, or to any team member — and we resolve it
> within 48 hours: re-clean free, repair free, or compensate. No forms, no
> arguing.

## Service charter (what customers can always expect)

- Transparent Naira pricing published before you book — weighed after, never
  charged before.
- Live tracking on every order, on the channel you choose (SMS, email,
  WhatsApp, Telegram).
- Free cancellation and rescheduling up to 2 hours before any pickup window.
- A human within reach: care team answers 7 AM – 10 PM daily, and bot-to-human
  handoff is one message away.
- Your preferences remembered and applied to every order, forever.

## Sustainability commitment

> We will run the most water- and energy-efficient laundry operation in
> Nigeria: 42% less water than home washing today, 100% biodegradable default
> detergents, a zero-perc dry cleaning fleet, recyclable packaging, and a
> public annual sustainability report from 2027.

## People commitment

> The best garment care in Lagos requires the best-treated team in Lagos:
> health coverage from day one, paid craft apprenticeships, schedules
> published three weeks ahead, quarterly profit sharing after one year, and
> promotion from within first — 92% of our leads started on the floor.

## Community commitment

> Clean clothes are dignity. Through our repair-and-rehome program we clean,
> mend, and donate customer garments to community partners across Lagos —
> 3,100+ pieces and counting — and offer permanent student pricing at Yaba.

## Elevator pitch (30 seconds)

> Lustra is Nigeria's premier tech-driven laundry platform. Smart self-service
> laundromats across Lagos you can reserve from your phone, pickup & delivery
> tracked to the minute, SLA-backed commercial laundry for hotels and
> shortlets, and a couture studio for everything from wedding dresses to
> aso-oke. One platform, one standard of care: like new, on time, every time.
